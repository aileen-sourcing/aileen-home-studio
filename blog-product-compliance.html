Understanding Product Compliance & Certifications for Global Imports

Published by Aileen • October 2026 Compliance & Regulations

Quick Summary for Buyers

Importing non-compliant goods causes customs holds and fines. Verifying target market certifications (such as CE, RoHS, or FCC) and utilizing DDP shipping ensures smooth import clearance.

Complying with national safety standards is crucial when entering international markets. Verifying essential certification documentation prior to order placement prevents unexpected customs clearance delays.

1. Key Standards by Market

  • EUROPE (EU) CE Marking, RoHS chemical restriction, and REACH safety compliance.
  • NORTH AMERICA FCC electromagnetic testing, UL/ETL safety, and CPSIA consumer standards.

2. Verifying Test Report Authenticity

Always request test reports issued by internationally accredited testing bodies (such as SGS, TÜV, Intertek, or BV) and cross-reference report numbers in official online databases.

3. Worry-Free Importation via DDP Terms

Choosing DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) shipping delegates customs filing and tariff payments to experienced logistics brokers, keeping your focus on sales rather than paperwork.
