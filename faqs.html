Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common inquiries regarding our products, customization, and sourcing services.

Products & Customization
Do you only supply the products listed on your website?
No. The items on our website serve as featured supply showcases. We are an integrated export partner with extensive factory networks in China. If you need products outside our online catalog—whether consumer goods, hardware, or raw materials—simply send us photos or specs for a fast quotation.
Do you support OEM/ODM and custom branding?
Yes, absolutely. We fully support custom specifications, material modifications, tailored packaging, and private labeling to align with your brand's unique market positioning.
What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
We support flexible order terms tailored to your business. We handle B2B volume wholesale with low MOQs, and also offer sample or small-batch orders for select product lines.
How do you handle quality control (QC) before shipping?
We conduct strict inspection protocols covering raw materials, production, and finished packaging. Every order undergoes quality check prior to dispatch to ensure full compliance with your specifications.
Supply Chain & Logistics
What logistics services do you provide?
We handle seamless end-to-end global supply chain integration. This includes factory-direct price negotiation, continuous production monitoring, complex customs clearance documentation, and reliable global freight forwarding/shipping coordination.
What trade terms and payment methods do you accept?
We accept secure payment options including PayPal, Credit Cards, and T/T Bank Transfers. For shipping, we offer flexible EXW, FOB, DAP, and door-to-door DDP options, handling customs clearance and logistics straight to your door.
What about the lead time for mass production?
It depends on the order quantity and the season you place the order. Generally speaking, we suggest that you start inquiry two months before the date you would like to get the products at your country.
