How We Manage Quality Control (QC) Before Shipping Overseas Orders

Published by Aileen • October 2026 Quality Assurance & Export

Quick Summary for Buyers

Unchecked quality defects lead to costly return logistics and customs delays. Our 4-stage Quality Control protocol—covering specification alignment, in-line checks, AQL pre-shipment inspection, and photo/video clearance—ensures your goods arrive exactly as approved.

For international B2B buyers, receiving products that fail to meet expected material or functional specifications can ruin seasonal sales campaigns. To safeguard every overseas order, we enforce a strict Quality Control (QC) framework across the entire production timeline.

1. Pre-Production Specification Alignment

Quality begins long before factories start mass assembly. Before production kickoff, we lock down all exact technical specs with the client:

  • CHECKPOINT Approved golden samples, Pantone color codes, and surface finish standards.
  • CHECKPOINT Structural weight tolerances, electrical parameter compliance, and barcode positioning.

2. In-Line Production Inspection

During active manufacturing, our team monitors raw material batches and machine settings to stop minor defects early before they multiply across large order quantities.

3. Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI) & AQL Audits

When mass production reaches 100% completion and at least 80% is packaged, we perform an inspection using international AQL (Acceptable Quality Limit) standards:

  • COSMETIC Checking for scratches, burrs, dirt, or shade variations.
  • FUNCTIONAL Mechanical load testing, electrical safety tests, and assembly fit checks.
  • PACKAGING Carton drop tests, barcode scannability, and shipping mark verification.

4. Transparent Photo & Video Approval

Prior to dispatching cargo to Chinese exit ports, we provide comprehensive photo and video inspection reports. Cargo is only released for export after client clearance.
