EXW vs. FOB vs. DDP: Which Shipping Term is Best for Importing from China?

Published by Aileen • October 2026 Export & Shipping Guide

Quick Summary for Buyers

Choosing the right trade term directly impacts your landed costs and logistics risks. In short: EXW offers max control if you have local forwarders; FOB is the standard for bulk ocean/air cargo; and DDP provides hassle-free door-to-door delivery with taxes included.

When sourcing goods from China, selecting the right Incoterms (International Commercial Terms) can significantly impact your total landing cost, shipping speed, and overall risk. Among the various shipping options available, EXW (Ex Works), FOB (Free on Board), and DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) are the three most common terms used by international buyers.

1. EXW (Ex Works): Factory Pickup Only

Under EXW terms, the seller makes the goods available at their factory or warehouse. As a buyer, you or your freight forwarder are responsible for everything else—including inland transit in China, export customs declaration, international freight, import duties, and final delivery.

  • BEST FOR Experienced buyers who already have a reliable freight forwarder in China and manage consolidated shipments from multiple suppliers.
  • KEY RISK High logistics coordination overhead and full responsibility for local Chinese export paperwork.

2. FOB (Free on Board): The Standard B2B Benchmark

FOB is the classic trade term for ocean and air freight shipments. The seller handles domestic inland logistics in China, export customs clearance, and loading the cargo onto the ship or aircraft. Once the goods pass the ship's rail, the risk and remaining shipping costs transfer to the buyer.

  • BEST FOR Established B2B importers ordering full container loads (FCL) or large LCL ocean shipments who prefer using their own freight forwarders.
  • KEY BENEFIT Clear cost separation and competitive port-to-port ocean freight rates.

3. DDP (Delivered Duty Paid): Hassle-Free Door-to-Door

Under DDP terms, the seller assumes full responsibility for delivering the cargo straight to your warehouse, store, or door. This includes international transport, export/import customs clearance, tariffs, taxes, and local door delivery.

  • BEST FOR Small-to-medium businesses, e-commerce sellers, or buyers who want a transparent "all-inclusive" price without dealing with freight forwarders or import customs brokers.
  • KEY BENEFIT Complete peace of mind with zero surprise import tax bills upon arrival.
