OEM vs. ODM: How to Customize Products & Packaging for Overseas Brands

Published by Aileen • October 2026 Product Customization & OEM

Quick Summary for Buyers

Building a private label requires choosing between OEM (custom manufacturing from your blueprints) and ODM (adding your logo and packaging to existing factory designs). Knowing which path to take saves upfront tooling investment and speeds up product launches.

Scaling an independent retail brand or e-commerce line relies heavily on distinct product presentation. Understanding the differences between OEM and ODM manufacturing helps you select the most efficient branding route in China.

1. OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer)

The factory builds products strictly according to your custom engineering drawings, CAD files, or technical specs. You hold full design rights and intellectual property.

  • BEST FOR Unique patented designs, proprietary functional features, or custom mold developments.

2. ODM (Original Design Manufacturer)

The factory supplies proven, pre-designed products. You simply apply your custom brand logo, color palette, and retail box packaging.

  • BEST FOR Fast time-to-market with minimal development cost and zero mold investment risk.
